What is ai?

Answer
----------------------------------------
**Short Definition:**
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

**Explanation:**
AI allows computers and machines to imitate human intelligence to automate tasks, make smart decisions, and create intelligent applications. Key capabilities and features of AI include:

* Learning from data and experience
* Reasoning and problem-solving
* Making decisions based on available information
* Understanding language
* Automating repetitive and complex tasks
